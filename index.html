ZlothY Dances v1.2.0
New
A fork/revamp of Colossal Emotes. Contains lots of dances and uses gorilla track networking to rotate the whole body just like body tracking.
How to use: Hold right trigger (B on PC) + left joystick to select. Release to play or change page. Left trigger to stop. Avoid spamming or switching emotes rapidly.
